Crude Oil Distillation Unit — Maintenance Reference

Section 1: Pump Maintenance

The centrifugal pump P-101A is a critical asset in the crude pre-heat circuit.
Bearing temperature alarm setpoint is 85 degrees Celsius. High vibration alert threshold is 7.1 mm/s RMS.
When abnormal vibration is detected, the unit should be inspected within 24 hours.
Oil analysis should be performed quarterly. Vibration signature analysis uses ISO 10816-3 as the reference standard.

Section 2: Compressor Operations

The reciprocating compressor K-201 handles overhead gas from the atmospheric distillation tower.
Discharge pressure normal operating range is 3.5 to 4.2 bar gauge.
Any deviation beyond this range triggers an automatic alarm in the DCS.
Compressor valve inspection interval is every 8,000 operating hours.

Section 3: Distillation Tower

The Atmospheric Distillation Tower (ADT) operates at near-atmospheric pressure.
Overhead temperature is controlled between 110 and 130 degrees Celsius.
The kerosene sidecut draw temperature is maintained at 190 to 210 degrees Celsius.
Tray differential pressure is monitored continuously. A delta-P rise above 0.15 bar across any tray section
indicates potential flooding or fouling and requires immediate operator attention.

Section 4: Safety Systems

The Safety Instrumented System (SIS) for the CDU includes the following Safety Instrumented Functions:
SIF-CDU-01: High furnace tube skin temperature trips the fuel gas supply.
SIF-CDU-02: High tower overhead pressure initiates pressure relief and controlled shutdown.
All SIS logic is implemented in a dedicated SIL-2 certified safety PLC.
Functional safety audits are conducted every five years.

Section 5: Predictive Maintenance Programme

The refinery uses a predictive maintenance programme covering vibration analysis, oil analysis,
infrared thermography, and ultrasonic thickness testing.
Key performance indicator: Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) for rotating equipment should exceed 18 months.
All maintenance activities require a valid Permit-to-Work (PTW) issued by the Area Operations Supervisor.
