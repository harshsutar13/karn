1. Purpose and Scope
Purpose: Guide evidence-based troubleshooting without allowing the AI to treat hypotheses as confirmed causes.
Scope: High vibration, elevated temperature, leakage, poor flow/head and abnormal noise.
2. Responsibilities
Operator/inspector records evidence; maintenance/engineering personnel review technical findings; authorized personnel approve actions. The AI workbench may extract, retrieve, calculate and draft but cannot self-approve.
3. Procedure
1. Register the abnormal symptom and preserve raw measurements.
2. Retrieve current inspection report, historical trends, operating SOP, maintenance history and manual sections.
3. Generate possible causes such as misalignment, bearing deterioration, lubrication issue, imbalance, cavitation, looseness or hydraulic operating condition.
4. For each possible cause, list supporting evidence, contradictory/missing evidence and required confirmation test.
5. Do not label a root cause 'confirmed' until the required human inspection/test evidence exists.
6. Prioritize immediate actions based on organization-approved safety/maintenance rules.
7. Generate an RCA draft and corrective-action list for human review.
4. Records
* Symptom
* Potential cause
* Evidence for
* Evidence against/missing
* Confirmation test
* Status: suspected/confirmed/rejected
5. AI Workbench Controls
* Use local models and local knowledge sources only in sovereign mode.
* Cite source document/revision/page or section when available.
* State missing evidence explicitly.
* Log selected model, agent steps, generated files and network status.
* Human review is mandatory for operational, maintenance and safety decisions.
