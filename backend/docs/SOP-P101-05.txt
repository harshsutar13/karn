1. Purpose and Scope
Purpose: Control AI-assisted drafting of maintenance approval and closure documentation.
Scope: P-101 corrective maintenance arising from inspection/RCA.
2. Responsibilities
Operator/inspector records evidence; maintenance/engineering personnel review technical findings; authorized personnel approve actions. The AI workbench may extract, retrieve, calculate and draft but cannot self-approve.
3. Procedure
1.	Create a case ID and link the inspection/RCA evidence.
2.	Retrieve the applicable approval workflow and previous P-101 correspondence.
3.	Draft an approval note stating findings, evidence, calculations, proposed scope and unresolved uncertainties.
4.	Do not invent cost, vendor, shutdown duration or approval authority when not present in the local knowledge base.
5.	Keep document status as Pending Human Review until an authorized reviewer acts.
6.	After approved maintenance, record work performed and post-maintenance checks.
7.	Close the case only after required evidence and human sign-off are present.
8.	Retain all versions, model-routing information and audit/network logs locally.
4. Records
•	Case ID
•	Approval note version
•	Reviewer
•	Decision
•	Work order
•	Completion evidence
•	Closure status
5. AI Workbench Controls
•	Use local models and local knowledge sources only in sovereign mode.
•	Cite source document/revision/page or section when available.
•	State missing evidence explicitly.
•	Log selected model, agent steps, generated files and network status.
•	Human review is mandatory for operational, maintenance and safety decisions.
