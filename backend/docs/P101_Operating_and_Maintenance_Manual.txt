4. Common Troubleshooting Matrix
* High vibration: investigate alignment, bearing condition, imbalance, looseness, cavitation/hydraulic condition.
* High bearing temperature: investigate lubrication, bearing condition, alignment/load and measurement validity.
* Seal leakage: inspect seal condition, alignment, operating condition and seal support arrangements where applicable.
* Low flow/head: inspect suction condition, valve lineup, blockage, air ingress, rotation/speed and pump condition.
5. Maintenance Principles
* Use organization-approved lockout/isolation procedures before intrusive work.
* Preserve as-found evidence before dismantling.
* Record parts replaced and measurements.
* After work, perform controlled restart and establish new baseline readings.
