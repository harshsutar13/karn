1. Purpose and Scope
Purpose: Define safe, repeatable startup, normal operation, monitoring and shutdown steps for the prototype pump.
Scope: P-101 and its immediate suction/discharge interfaces in the synthetic cooling-water service.
2. Responsibilities
Operator/inspector records evidence; maintenance/engineering personnel review technical findings; authorized personnel approve actions. The AI workbench may extract, retrieve, calculate and draft but cannot self-approve.
3. Procedure
1. Verify work area is safe and maintenance clearance is released.
2. Confirm suction source is available and suction valve is open.
3. Confirm pump casing/suction line are primed and vented as applicable.
4. Verify lubrication condition and no obvious leakage/loose parts.
5. Confirm coupling guard is fitted.
6. Start P-101 using the approved local operating method.
7. Confirm discharge develops and gradually establish required flow.
8. Record suction pressure, discharge pressure, flow, bearing temperature, vibration and seal condition.
9. During normal operation, investigate abnormal noise, cavitation symptoms, leakage, vibration or temperature rise.
10. For normal shutdown, reduce load as applicable, stop pump and place valves/equipment in the approved standby condition.
11. For suspected unsafe condition, stop/escalate according to the organization's actual emergency procedure; AI must not invent shutdown limits.
4. Records
* Start/stop time
* Flow
* Suction/discharge pressure
* Vibration
* Bearing temperature
* Seal condition
* Operator remarks
5. AI Workbench Controls
* Use local models and local knowledge sources only in sovereign mode.
* Cite source document/revision/page or section when available.
* State missing evidence explicitly.
* Log selected model, agent steps, generated files and network status.
* Human review is mandatory for operational, maintenance and safety decisions.
