1. Purpose and Scope
Purpose: Define the inspection points used by the multimodal AI demonstration.
Scope: Routine visual and condition inspection of P-101.
2. Responsibilities
Operator/inspector records evidence; maintenance/engineering personnel review technical findings; authorized personnel approve actions. The AI workbench may extract, retrieve, calculate and draft but cannot self-approve.
3. Procedure
1. Confirm equipment tag P-101 and inspection date.
2. Capture overall equipment photograph and close-up evidence for abnormal findings.
3. Inspect seal area for leakage.
4. Observe noise and signs of cavitation.
5. Measure vibration at defined bearing locations using the organization's approved instrument/method.
6. Measure bearing housing temperature.
7. Inspect coupling guard, baseplate, visible fasteners and foundation condition.
8. Record suction/discharge indications and operating flow where available.
9. Compare results only with approved local limits. Prototype classifications are demonstration-only.
10. Create findings with evidence, severity, proposed follow-up and reviewer status.
4. Records
* Photo IDs
* Instrument IDs
* Measurements
* Finding severity
* Evidence reference
* Reviewer decision
5. AI Workbench Controls
* Use local models and local knowledge sources only in sovereign mode.
* Cite source document/revision/page or section when available.
* State missing evidence explicitly.
* Log selected model, agent steps, generated files and network status.
* Human review is mandatory for operational, maintenance and safety decisions.
