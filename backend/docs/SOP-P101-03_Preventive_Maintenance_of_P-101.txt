1. Purpose and Scope
Purpose: Provide a prototype maintenance schedule and work sequence.
Scope: Routine preventive maintenance for P-101.
2. Responsibilities
Operator/inspector records evidence; maintenance/engineering personnel review technical findings; authorized personnel approve actions. The AI workbench may extract, retrieve, calculate and draft but cannot self-approve.
3. Procedure
1. Daily/shift: review leakage, noise, vibration trend, temperature trend and operating indications.
2. Weekly: inspect accessible fasteners, guards, housekeeping and obvious piping/support issues.
3. Monthly: review vibration/temperature trends and compare with prior records.
4. Quarterly/demo interval: verify alignment condition, lubrication condition and coupling condition as permitted by the actual maintenance program.
5. Planned shutdown: inspect bearings, mechanical seal, coupling and rotating condition as required by approved manual.
6. After maintenance, perform controlled restart and record baseline vibration/temperature.
7. Update maintenance history and close actions only after human verification.
4. Records
* Maintenance date
* Work order
* Components inspected
* Parts changed
* As-found/as-left condition
* Post-maintenance baseline
5. AI Workbench Controls
* Use local models and local knowledge sources only in sovereign mode.
* Cite source document/revision/page or section when available.
* State missing evidence explicitly.
* Log selected model, agent steps, generated files and network status.
* Human review is mandatory for operational, maintenance and safety decisions.
